What I built

  • Integrated two UR5e arms and a Fanuc Delta manipulator into a coordinated multi-robot sorting cell.
  • Programmed an Arduino-based PLC in structured text to orchestrate control across all three robots.
  • Used vision cameras to detect and localize randomized parts for automated pick and place.
  • Synchronized conveyors and sensors to palletize sorted parts into a structured grid.
